# Sample image sources

These photographs illustrate saffron and do not represent the company’s stock. The photograph of threads is generic and does not identify a specific cut.

- `saffron-threads.webp`: Rainer Zenz, [Iran saffron threads](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Iran_saffron_threads.jpg), public domain. Resized and encoded as WebP.
- `saffron-flowers.webp` and `saffron-flowers-760.webp`: Merve Safa, [Saffron Flowers](https://www.pexels.com/photo/saffron-flowers-crocus-sativus-14411011/), [Pexels license](https://www.pexels.com/license/). Resized and encoded as WebP.
- `saffron-field.webp`: [Saffron Flower in Pampore](https://www.pexels.com/photo/vivid-saffron-flower-blooming-in-pampore-fields-29607044/), [Pexels license](https://www.pexels.com/license/). Resized and encoded as WebP.
- `editorial-saffron.webp` and `editorial-saffron-780.webp`: generated editorial photograph for this preview.

The purchasing flow was informed by [RosaTam’s quotation page](https://www.rosatam.com/request-quotation), [Saffron Distribution’s wholesale catalogue](https://saffrondistribution.com/en/collections/ingrosso) and [Saffrona’s B2B page](https://saffrona.eu/en/b2b-saffron-supplier/). The site’s copy was written for this project.
